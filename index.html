About Our Church

Learn about our history, mission, and beliefs

Our History

Seattle International Church (SIC) was established to provide a vibrant, spirit-filled worship atmosphere specifically designed for the diverse and multi-ethnic community at Bugema University. Over the years, it has grown from a small gathering of students and faculty members into a prominent international spiritual hub.

As a Seventh-day Adventist congregation, we celebrate diversity and strive to be a home away from home for students representing dozens of nations, fostering deep spiritual integration and professional excellence under God's guidance.

Our Mission

To proclaim the everlasting gospel of Jesus Christ and prepare people for His soon return.

Our Vision

To build an international family of believers that reflects Christ's love through worship, discipleship, and service.

Core Values

Biblical Truth

We hold the Word of God as our ultimate standard of faith and practice.

Prayer

We believe prayer is the lifeline of our relationship with God and the foundation of all ministry.

Love

We practice the unconditional love of Jesus in our relationships and community interaction.

Service

We follow Christ's example of humble service to meet the physical and spiritual needs of others.

Fellowship

We cultivate a warm, inclusive international family where everyone belongs.

Integrity

We strive to walk in honesty, transparency, and consistency in our words and actions.
